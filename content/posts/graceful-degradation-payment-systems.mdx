## The failure that matters most

A payment provider timing out shouldn't take checkout down entirely. Most outages I've seen weren't caused by a provider being fully down — they were caused by a provider being *slow*, and the calling system having no timeout or fallback path.

## The design

Every provider call has an aggressive timeout and a circuit breaker. Once a provider trips the breaker, new requests route to a secondary provider or queue for retry, instead of piling up behind a slow dependency and taking the whole request path down with it.

## Why this is a design decision, not a bug fix

Graceful degradation has to be planned at the architecture stage — which requests can queue, which must fail fast and visibly, and which have a safe fallback. Bolting this on after an incident is far more expensive than designing for it up front.
