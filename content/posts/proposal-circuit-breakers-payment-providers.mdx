## Current state

Outbound calls to third-party payment providers have no circuit breaker. A slow provider currently degrades the entire checkout path, since requests queue up behind it with no fast-fail mechanism.

## Proposal

Wrap every provider call in a circuit breaker with an aggressive timeout. After a threshold of failures or timeouts, the breaker opens and routes new requests to a fallback provider or a "try again shortly" response, instead of letting them queue.

## Expected impact

Provider slowness becomes isolated to that provider's traffic instead of degrading checkout site-wide. Recovery is also faster — a half-open breaker can test the provider periodically instead of waiting for a manual intervention.

## Cost

One additional dependency (a circuit breaker library) and some new failure-path testing. Small compared to the blast radius of the current all-or-nothing behavior.
