## The habit

Before requesting review, read the diff as if you were the one being asked "why did you do it this way" in an interview — not "does this look right," but "can I defend every decision in here out loud."

## What it actually catches

Not syntax errors — those get caught by linters anyway. It catches the decisions made on autopilot: an error case silently swallowed, a magic number with no explanation, an edge case that "probably won't happen." Those are exactly the things an interviewer, or a sharp reviewer, will ask about.

## Why it matters beyond the PR itself

It's the same muscle a system design interview exercises: can you explain the *why* behind a decision, not just describe the *what*. Practicing it on real work is more useful than practicing it only under interview pressure.
