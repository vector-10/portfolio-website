## It's rarely the diagram that fails

Most candidates can draw a reasonable high-level architecture. Where interviews actually diverge is the follow-up: "what happens when this component goes down," "how do you handle two of these arriving at the same time," "why this database over that one, specifically for this access pattern."

## The questions that kept coming back

- How do you handle a duplicate request from a retry?
- What's your consistency model, and did you choose it deliberately or by default?
- Where's the single point of failure in what you just drew, and what's the blast radius if it fails?

## What helped

Treating every design as if a skeptical senior engineer was going to ask "what breaks first" — and having an actual answer, not a hand-wave — mattered more than the initial diagram being impressive.
