summary:
  problem: >-
    For modern platforms with multiple users like logistics apps with thousands
    of drivers, distributors, and merchants, managing money is an absolute
    nightmare. Trying to manually track who paid what, route earnings to the
    right person, and handle bank transfers usually leads to broken accounting
    ledger balances, missed payments, and massive operational leaks. On top of
    that, verifying user identities to comply with Central Bank rules creates a
    massive friction point that slows down onboarding.
  did: >-
    I built Kanall, a virtual bank account infrastructure that automatically
    gives every user, merchant, or driver their own dedicated Nigerian bank
    account through a secure API integration. I engineered a strict, bulletproof
    double entry accounting ledger where every single movement of money is
    permanent, unchangeable, and perfectly balanced to zero. To make it
    completely foolproof, I built a multi layered confirmation system that
    catches and verifies payments across three timelines: instant checks,
    background sweeps, and a 24 hour master audit.
  result: >-
    Platforms can now onboard users instantly with automatic, three tier
    identity verification while completely automating their financial
    operations. Because every entity gets a dedicated account number, money
    tracking and reconciliations happen instantly with zero human errors or
    duplicate payment glitches. This gives platform managers perfect visibility
    over balances, automated outbound payouts, and flawless compliance logs,
    giving them every reason to confidently scale their operations without
    financial blind spots.
